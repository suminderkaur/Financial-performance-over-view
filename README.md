# Financial-performance-overview
An interactive Power BI project designed to track global sales trajectories, analyze product profitability, and identify seasonal revenue patterns across multiple business segments.

# ❓ The Business Problem
We were generating high sales volumes across multiple countries, but it was incredibly difficult to see where our money was actually coming from—or where we were losing it. 

Because our data was scattered, we didn't know which products were our top drivers, which countries yielded the highest profit margins, or if our heavy discounting strategies were hurting our bottom line. We also lacked a clear view of our seasonal peaks and valleys, making inventory planning a guessing game. 

This project solves that by bringing all our financial data into a centralized, interactive dashboard to enable data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🖥️ Dashboard Structure

The report is split into three focused pages to give stakeholders both high-level summaries and deep-dive analytics:

### 1. Financial Performance Overview
* **Focus:** Overall financial health and historical tracking.
* **Key Visuals:** KPI blocks for core financial indicators, monthly profit trend line, and a YoY growth trajectory chart.

### 2. Regional & Global Performance
* **Focus:** Geographic sales and business segment efficiency.
* **Key Visuals:** Global interactive map tracking profit locations, profit margin breakdowns by segment, and country-level profitability rankings.

### 3. Product Analytics
* **Focus:** Inventory traction, volume metrics, and promotional impact.
* **Key Visuals:** Flagship product highlight, sales vs. volume comparisons, and average discount distribution charts.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

* **Explosive Growth:** Total profit grew by **233.81%** over the tracked timeline, resulting in **$118.7M** in total sales and **$16.9M** in net profit.
* **The High-Margin Engine:** The **Channel Partners** business segment is our most efficient operation, delivering an outstanding **73% profit margin**.
* **The Revenue Drain:** The **Enterprise** segment operates at a **negative profit margin (-3%)**, proving that our largest corporate deals are currently losing money.
* **Geographic Pillars:** **Germany** and **France** lead global efficiency with stable **16% profit margins**, while the US trails the group at 12%.
* **Strong Holiday Seasonality:** **October** represents our massive annual demand peak (**$3.4M in profit**), which is immediately followed by a steep contraction in **November ($1.4M)**.
* **Flagship Product:** **Paseo** is our undisputed highest-selling item, completely dominating overall volume and revenue generation.
